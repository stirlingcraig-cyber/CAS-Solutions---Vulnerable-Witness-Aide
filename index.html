Overview

Five stages, one continuous account

An aide-memoire for planning interviews with adult and deemed vulnerable witnesses. It combines trauma-informed free recall (Tell, Explain, Describe) with the eight ADVOKATE limbs for identification evidence, and captures what the Procurator Fiscal needs to consider special measures.

PEACE

The stages follow the PEACE model used by Police Scotland: Plan and prepare and Engage and explain (stage 1), Account (stages 2 to 4), Closure (stage 5), then Evaluate the interview afterwards.

Child witness

Witnesses under 18 should be interviewed through the Scottish Child Interview Model (Joint Investigative Interview) by trained police and social work interviewers. Do not use this aide as a substitute for that process.

Unofficial

This is a planning aid, not force policy. Check wording and legal references against current Police Scotland SOPs and COPFS guidance before operational use. Keep personal data out of any AI tool that is not approved for it.

Stage 2

      
Cognitive free recall

      
Get the witness's own uninterrupted account first. Use open TED prompts, allow silence, and do not introduce details the witness has not given.

      

        
T
Tell me
Opens the account. Use at the start and to move to a new topic.

        
E
Explain to me
Expands on something the witness has already said, in their words.

        
D
Describe for me
Draws out sensory and physical detail of a person, place or object.

Research basis

          
Professor Becky Milne and the Cognitive Interview

          
Professor Becky Milne, Professor of Forensic Psychology at the University of Portsmouth, is one of the leading researchers on interviewing witnesses and victims. Her work tested the Cognitive Interview with vulnerable groups, including adults with learning disabilities, and helped shape national practice on interviewing vulnerable and intimidated witnesses.

          
    
            
  • The Cognitive Interview reliably increases the amount of correct detail recalled, with little or no rise in errors.
    • 
            
  • Adults with mild learning disabilities recall more with the Cognitive Interview, but remain more open to leading and misleading questions. Question style matters as much as technique.
    • 
            
  • Techniques should be simplified for vulnerable witnesses. Context reinstatement and "report everything" carry most of the benefit; "change perspective" is generally left out.
    • 
            
  • Co-developed Achieving Best Evidence guidance on vulnerable and intimidated witnesses, and contributed to the tiered PEACE interviewing framework.
    •

Stage 3

      
The ADVOKATE matrix

      
Eight limbs that test the quality of an identification. Cover each one with open questions after free recall, and record the answer in the witness's own words so the evidence holds up under cross-examination.

      

      

    


    
    

      
Stage 4

      
Vulnerability & special measures

      
Record fears and observed reactions accurately. This material supports the Procurator Fiscal's decision on a vulnerable witness notice or application. The PF and the court decide which measures apply.

      

      
Special measures under the Criminal Procedure (Scotland) Act 1995

      

        
MeasureSectionWhat it means for the witnessStandard?

        
      

      
Deemed vulnerable
Complainers in sexual offences, human trafficking, stalking and domestic abuse cases are deemed vulnerable under s271(1) and are entitled to the standard special measures. Confirm the current position with the PF for each case.

    


    
    

      
Stage 5

      
Closing & reassurance

      
End on a neutral, supportive footing. Explain what happens next without predicting outcomes or making promises about court.

      

    


    
    

      
Serious crime

      
Murder, attempted murder, kidnap & extortion

      
Extra protocols for major and life-at-risk investigations. In each case the Senior Investigating Officer's witness interview strategy takes priority over this aide.

RIPSA risk
Never ask a witness to go back and find something out, keep a conversation going, or record contact for you. Doing so may make them a covert human intelligence source under s1(7) RIPSA, which needs authorisation under s7. Vulnerable people should be used as a source only in the most exceptional circumstances.
