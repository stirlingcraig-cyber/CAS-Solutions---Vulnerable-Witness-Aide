# CAS Solutions · Vulnerable Witness Aide

An unofficial, single-page aide-memoire for planning interviews with vulnerable adult witnesses in Scotland.

## Features

- **Search** across every question, ADVOKATE limb and legal reference. Press `/` to focus, use the arrow keys and Enter to jump.
- **AI prompt builder**: choose the witness profile, support needs and offence type to generate a case-specific prompt, with flags for deemed-vulnerable offences, child witnesses and possible personal data.
- **Five interview stages** mapped to PEACE: preparation and ground rules, TED free recall, the ADVOKATE matrix, vulnerability and special measures, closing.
- **Cognitive Interview recall aids** with scripted wording, drawing on the research of Professor Becky Milne (University of Portsmouth).
- **Serious crime protocols** for murder, attempted murder, kidnap and extortion.
- **Policy and procedure** notes on legacy ACPOS guidance, RIPSA 2000 (CHIS and directed surveillance) and Police Scotland practice.
- **Pre-interview checklist**, saved in your own browser only.
- Copy buttons on every question, light and dark themes, and a phone-friendly layout.

## Run it

Open `index.html` in any modern browser. There is no build step and no dependencies. Fonts load from Google Fonts, and system fonts are used if they are unavailable.

GitHub Pages sites are public, even from a private repository on most plans. Do not add case data or personal information to the repository.

## Important

This is a planning aid, not Police Scotland or COPFS policy, and it is not endorsed by either. Check wording and legal references against current SOPs and COPFS guidance before operational use. Witnesses under 18 should be interviewed through the Scottish Child Interview Model. Only paste prompts into AI tools approved for the information involved.
